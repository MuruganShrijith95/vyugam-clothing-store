# VYUGAM Growth Playbook — Selling before Diwali

A 30-day plan to take a live storefront with 21 products and no ad budget into the
festive season, built around what the business actually has: WhatsApp ordering, UPI on
its own VPA, and a ₹399 entry price.

- **Catalogue** — 21 SKUs, ₹399–₹9,999
- **Orders via** — WhatsApp 7550371346
- **Payments** — UPI 7550371346@ybl, plus COD

## 00 · What "viral" can and cannot be bought

**Nobody can guarantee a viral post** — not an agency, not a strategy deck, not this
document. What actually produces breakout content is volume plus a repeatable hook, and
the honest arithmetic is that roughly 1 in 30 to 1 in 50 reels breaks out for a new
account. This plan is built to give 60 shots in 30 days rather than 6 brilliant ones.

The part fully under our control is **conversion**. At this scale, 400 engaged local
followers who trust the brand outsell 40,000 scattered ones. Everything here optimises
for orders landing in WhatsApp, not for a number on a profile.

Three constraints shape every decision below:

1. **No payment gateway** — every order is a human conversation. Slow, but it converts
   far better than a checkout page.
2. **No ad budget to waste** — organic has to carry the first three weeks.
3. **Five to nine weeks from the biggest retail window of the Indian year** — the work
   done in September is what sells in November.

## 01 · Positioning

> Handloom and hand-block Indian wear, direct from the weave, starting at ₹399 —
> ordered on WhatsApp, delivered across India.

Use that line in the Instagram bio, the WhatsApp Business description, every ad, and the
first comment of every reel. Consistency beats cleverness: people need to see the same
claim four or five times before it registers as a brand rather than a post.

### Why not "premium ethnic wear"

"Premium" is what every ethnic brand in India already says, and it starts a fight against
Fabindia and Manyavar that cannot be won on this budget. The defensible ground is
**provenance plus price** — we can name the weave, the town and the technique on every
product, and we open at ₹399 where most heritage brands open at ₹2,000. That combination
is unusual and it is true, which makes it easy to talk about for a year without running
out of material.

### The offer ladder the catalogue now supports

| Rung | Price | Product | Job it does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Entry | ₹399 | Handloom Cotton Essential Tee | Converts a stranger into a customer. Nobody agonises over ₹399. |
| Habit | ₹899–₹1,499 | Georgette saree, chikankari kurta, khadi jacket, co-ord set | The repeat-purchase band. This is where margin lives. |
| Festive | ₹2,499–₹2,899 | Chanderi kurta set, bandhani lehenga | The October–November spike. Marketed from mid-September. |
| Anchor | ₹5,999–₹9,999 | Banarasi silk, bandhgala | Rarely sold. Makes ₹2,899 feel reasonable by comparison. |

The ₹399 tee is the single most important SKU we own, and not because of its margin. It
is the cheapest way to turn a follower into someone with a delivery address saved in a
WhatsApp thread — and that thread is the asset we sell into every festival for the next
three years.

## 02 · The 30-day sprint

Assumes one person with a phone, working roughly two hours a day. It front-loads the
unglamorous setup because everything after it compounds.

### Week 0 — the four days before posting anything

- Switch Instagram to a **Business account** and connect a Facebook Page (required for
  ads later, painful to retrofit).
- Install **WhatsApp Business**, not personal WhatsApp. Set the catalogue, greeting
  message, away message, and quick replies for the five questions asked hourly: price,
  size, delivery time, COD, fabric.
- Shoot **one day of content covering three weeks** — natural light near a window, a
  plain wall, a phone on any stable surface. Aim for 30 clips of 10–20 seconds. This
  single day is the difference between posting daily and quietly giving up in week two.
- Write the bio: positioning line, "📍 Ships across India", and `wa.me/917550371346` as
  the one link.
- Seed the grid with **nine posts before inviting anybody**. An empty profile converts
  nothing.

### Weeks 1–4

| Week | Focus | Output | Target by Sunday |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1 — Proof** | Show the product being real. Fabric close-ups, the weave, block-print registration marks, packing an order. | 2 reels/day, 1 story set/day | First 5 orders, 150 followers |
| **2 — Utility** | Teach something. Draping, sizing, care, "how to spot real chikankari". Utility gets saved, and saves are what the algorithm rewards. | 2 reels/day, 1 carousel/day | 15 orders, 500 followers |
| **3 — Social proof** | Customer photos, unboxing clips, review screenshots. Launch the referral code. Send the first 10 barter parcels. | 2 reels/day, 3 UGC reposts/wk | 35 orders, 1,200 followers |
| **4 — Scale what worked** | Put money behind the two best organic reels. Open the festive pre-book. Do not create new ad creative. | 2 reels/day, ₹500/day ads | 70 orders, 2,500 followers |

Those targets are a realistic ceiling for a new account posting twice daily with no
existing audience, not a promise. Hitting half of them is fine; the compounding matters
more than month one.

## 03 · Content engine — five pillars

Rotate in order. Never sit down wondering what to post — the pillar decides, and the
day's shoot list comes from our own catalogue.

| Pillar | What it looks like | Why it works | Share |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The Weave** | Macro shots of Chanderi transparency, bandhani knots, block-print seams. Slow pans, no talking. | Nobody else is showing this. It is our proof of provenance. | 30% |
| **The Drape** | Six ways to drape one saree; styling a kurta three ways; the jacket over a plain tee. | Saved and re-watched. Saves outrank likes. | 25% |
| **The Price** | "₹399 vs ₹3,999 — what actually changes." Honest comparisons, no fake competitor bashing. | Price content travels furthest in Indian fashion. | 20% |
| **The People** | Customer photos, packing orders, replying on WhatsApp, the person behind the brand. | Converts. People buy from a person, especially without a gateway. | 15% |
| **The Festival** | Navratri colour of the day, Karva Chauth reds, Diwali gifting sets. | Rides search and hashtag spikes we did not have to create. | 10% |

**Rhythm:** reels 2×/day at 12pm and 8pm IST; stories 5–7 frames daily; carousel 3×/week;
WhatsApp broadcast twice a week, never more. Reply to every comment within the first
hour — early engagement decides how far a reel travels and costs nothing.

## 04 · Twelve reel scripts, ready to film

The hook is the first two seconds and it is 80% of the outcome.

| Hook (0–2s, on screen) | The middle | Ends on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "This saree costs ₹899. Here's why it isn't cheap." | Georgette drape test, satin border, pleats holding after eight hours. | "Link in bio. Or just WhatsApp us." |
| "You're being sold printed chikankari. Here's how to tell." | Turn the Lucknowi kurta inside out — real bakhiya shadow work sits on the reverse. Compare to a printed one. | "Ours is ₹1,199. Real." |
| "₹399. That's it. That's the tee." | Three ways to wear it — under the Nehru jacket, under the open linen shirt, alone. | "Buy three. Seriously." |
| "Six drapes. One saree. Ninety seconds." | Rapid cuts: Nivi, Bengali, Gujarati, pant-style, belted, cape. | "Save this before Diwali." |
| "POV: the sangeet runs till 2am and your lehenga weighs 4kg." | Cut to the bandhani lehenga on georgette. Twirl. Show the weight on a kitchen scale. | "Under 900 grams. ₹2,899." |
| "Anarkali. With pockets." | Slow reveal — hands into concealed side pockets, phone in, twirl. | "Why did this take until 2026?" |
| "What ₹2,499 of real Chanderi looks like against a window." | Hold the fabric to daylight. The glassy transparency no blend reproduces. | "Handwoven in Madhya Pradesh." |
| "Every man owns one boring kurta. Fix it in ₹1,499." | Plain white kurta, then the khadi Nehru jacket over it. Before/after in one cut. | "Fastest upgrade you can buy." |
| "Block print vs machine print — look at the corners." | Macro on the Sanganeri co-ord where blocks meet and slightly overlap. Machine prints never do that. | "Hand-printed in Sanganer." |
| "Packing your order at 11pm." | Silent, ASMR-ish: fold, tissue, tape, address label, the WhatsApp thread on screen. | "Yours ships tomorrow." |
| "Navratri day 3 is green. We have three." | Nine-day colour calendar, our SKUs mapped to each day. | "Save the full nine." |
| "Reading your WhatsApp messages out loud." | Screen-record real (permission-asked) customer messages, react honestly — including a complaint we fixed. | "We reply in ten minutes." |

Two rules that matter more than the scripts: put the hook **on screen as text**, not just
in the voiceover, because most people watch on mute. And **never delete a reel that
flopped** — the account-level signal from deleting is worse than a bad post sitting
quietly in the grid.

## 06 · WhatsApp — the highest-converting channel

With no payment gateway, WhatsApp is not a support channel, it is the checkout. Reply
speed is a product feature: under ten minutes during waking hours will out-convert any
website optimisation available to us.

### Order confirmation — send within 5 minutes, every time

```text

Ilkal Handloom Cotton Saree — Sage Green
₹1,299 · Free delivery
```

### The follow-up that recovers the most money

Someone asked a question and went quiet. Do not send "just following up" — send the
specific thing they were worried about.

```text
Hi! You were asking about sizing on the Chanderi set 🙂

Quick answer: it runs true to size, and the pants are fully lined. If you're between
sizes, take the larger — the kurta is meant to fall loose.

We have 4 left in M. Want me to hold one for you till tomorrow?
```


## 07 · Three viral loops

Individual posts are lottery tickets. Loops are the part where each customer brings the
next one, and they keep working after we stop posting.

**1 · Referral, run entirely in WhatsApp.** Every customer gets a code — their own first
name works and is easier to remember than a random string. Their friend gets **₹150 off**
a first order; they get **₹150 credited** on their next. No app, no plugin, no tracking
pixel: match the code manually, because at this volume we can and the personal touch is
the point.

**2 · The UGC ask, timed to arrival.** Ask **three days after delivery**, never at
dispatch:

```text
Hope the saree arrived well 🙂

If you wear it somewhere, would you send us one photo? We repost customers every Friday,
and we'll send you a ₹200 credit whether we use it or not.
```

Reposted customers become repeat customers at a noticeably higher rate, and every repost
is content we did not have to shoot.

**3 · The giveaway that builds a list, not vanity followers.** Standard "tag 3 friends"
giveaways attract people who unfollow the moment it ends. Instead: prize is a ₹2,899
bandhani lehenga, entry is **a comment naming the festival they'd wear it to**, plus a
follow. The comments become genuine engagement rather than tag-spam, the responses tell
us which occasion to build the next collection around, and everyone who commented is a
warm audience.

| Lever | Cost |
| --- | --- |
| Referral discount | ₹150 both sides |
| UGC credit | ₹200 per photo sent |
| Giveaway | ₹2,899, one lehenga at cost |
| Ads, week 4 | ₹500/day, proven creative only |

## 08 · Creators and ads

### Micro-creator barter, not paid influencers

Creators with **5,000–25,000 followers** in our own city will take product for content.
Above 50,000 they want cash and their audience is too diffuse to convert. Send ten
parcels; expect three to post, one to post well, and treat the other six as cost of doing
business.

| Tier | What we send | What we ask for | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 5k–10k | Tee + co-ord set | 1 reel, 2 stories, keep the product | ~₹950 |
| 10k–25k | Chikankari kurta or kurta set | 1 reel + usage rights for our ads | ~₹1,600 |
| Wedding creators | Bandhani lehenga, loaned | Sangeet footage, returned after | ₹0 if returned |

**Always ask for usage rights.** A creator's reel that performs organically becomes our
best-converting ad, and re-shooting it ourselves never works as well.

### Paid ads — not before week 4

Never run ads against creative that has not already proved itself organically. Take the
two reels with the highest save rate, put ₹500/day behind them as Instagram-only traffic
ads pointing at the WhatsApp link, and target women 24–40 in the top three delivery
cities. Kill anything above **₹40 cost per WhatsApp conversation started** after three
days; scale anything under ₹20.

One number decides whether ads are worth running at all: **margin per order**. If a
₹1,299 saree nets ₹400 after cost and shipping, we can afford roughly ₹150 to acquire
that customer and still be fine — provided they come back. If they never come back, ads
at any price are a slow way to lose money, which is exactly why the WhatsApp list and the
referral loop come first.

## 09 · Festive calendar

Indian festive retail is front-loaded: people buy two to three weeks *before* the date,
not on it. Every campaign therefore starts well ahead of the festival it targets.

| Window | Occasion | Push | Start selling |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mid Sept | Pre-festive build | Provenance content, list building, ₹399 entry offers | Now |
| Late Sept | Festive pre-book opens | Hold-your-size campaign on Chanderi and bandhani | Now |
| Mid Oct | Navratri (9 nights) | Colour-of-the-day series — nine days of guaranteed content | Late Sept |
| Late Oct | Karva Chauth | Reds and maroons; gifting angle aimed at husbands | Mid Oct |
| Early Nov | Dhanteras → Diwali | Peak. Gifting sets, family-matching, last-dispatch countdown | Mid Oct |
| Late Nov | Wedding season opens | Lehenga and sherwani push; sangeet-specific content | Early Nov |

Festival dates shift with the lunar calendar — confirm this year's exact dates against a
panchang before scheduling, and set the last-dispatch date at least four days before
Diwali itself.

## 10 · Scorecard — five numbers, every Sunday

Ignore follower count as a goal. Four of these come straight out of WhatsApp.

| Metric | Where to find it | Month 1 target | What it tells us |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| WhatsApp conversations started | Chat list, new threads | 120 | Whether content drives intent at all |

| Reel save rate | Instagram insights | >2% | Best predictor of reach. Optimise for this, not likes |
| Repeat rate, 60 days | Names appearing twice | 15% | Whether we have a business or a series of one-off sales |
